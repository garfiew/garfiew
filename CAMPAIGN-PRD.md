# Campaign PRD — Skinless Foundation
## "No More Make Up, Skinless Foundation is New Skin"

**Status:** Ready for execution  
**Budget:** 50,000 THB / 1 month  
**Platforms:** Meta (FB + IG) + TikTok  
**Channels:** Meta Shop, Shopee, Lazada (no website)

---

## Problem Statement

The client has a beauty product — Skinless Foundation — with a bold positioning claim ("Skinless Foundation is New Skin") but no structured plan to communicate it across Meta and TikTok within a 50,000 THB monthly budget. The target audience (women 20–35 + LGBTQ+ makeup community) currently believes foundation is a cosmetic layer applied on top of skin — a routine step, not a skin upgrade. Without a content action plan, the brand risks producing disconnected posts that generate impressions but fail to shift this belief or drive purchases on Meta Shop, Shopee, and Lazada.

---

## Solution

A 1-month content action plan built around a single strategic insight — delivered through 4 content pillars, platform-specific formats for Meta and TikTok, a paid/organic budget split, and a weekly content calendar. All conversion flows directly into Meta Shop, Shopee, and Lazada with zero dependency on a website.

**Strategic Insight:**  
The audience is tired of performing beauty. They want to feel like themselves — just the best version. Skinless Foundation gives them permission to stop hiding and start enhancing.

## Implementation Decisions

### 1. Content Pillar Framework (4 Pillars)

| Pillar | Theme | % of Content | Audience Job |
|--------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Liberation** | "No more rules, no more heavy coverage" | 25% | Shift belief: permission to simplify beauty |
| **Proof** | Real demos, before/after, shade range, skin tones | 35% | Build credibility and reduce purchase risk |
| **Community** | LGBTQ+ voices, user-generated content, diversity | 20% | Build belonging and trust for non-mainstream audience |
| **Conversion** | Product features, price-value, where to buy, limited offers | 20% | Drive to Meta Shop / Shopee / Lazada |

### 2. Platform Strategy — Roles in the Funnel

**TikTok: Awareness + Viral Discovery**
- Role: Reach cold audiences via algorithm; generate organic reach at low cost
- Primary formats: 15–30 sec Reels (hook in first 2 sec), duet/stitch hooks, "POV" storytelling, transformation demos
- Tone: Raw, authentic, educational-entertainment ("edutainment"), creator-style — not polished brand ads
- Posting frequency: 5–7 organic posts/week + 2 paid TopView or In-Feed Ads/week
- Conversion: Bio link → Shopee/Lazada product page; TikTok Shop if available

**Meta (Instagram + Facebook): Consideration + Conversion**
- Role: Retarget TikTok audience; drive purchase intent to Meta Shop and external marketplaces
- IG Reels: Repurpose top TikTok content (trimmed + captioned for IG) — 3–4/week
- IG Feed: High-quality product photography + lifestyle — 2–3/week
- IG/FB Stories: Polls, swipe-up links to Shopee/Lazada, countdown timers, testimonials — daily
- FB Feed: Paid conversion ads targeting 20–35 women + LGBTQ+ interest segments
- Conversion: Meta Shop primary; Shopee/Lazada via link in bio + Stories swipe-up

### 3. Budget Allocation (50,000 THB)

| Channel | Amount (THB) | % | Objective |
|---------|-------------|---|-----------|
| Meta Paid Ads | 27,500 | 55% | Conversion (Shopee/Lazada/Meta Shop) + Retargeting |
| TikTok Paid Ads | 12,500 | 25% | Awareness + Reach (In-Feed Ads) |
| Content Production | 8,000 | 16% | Video shooting, editing, photo assets |
| Creative Reserve | 2,000 | 4% | Boosting high-performing organic posts mid-month |

**Meta sub-allocation (27,500 THB):**
- Traffic/Conversion campaigns → Shopee + Lazada links: 16,500 THB
- Meta Shop catalog ads: 7,000 THB
- Retargeting (video viewers + profile visitors): 4,000 THB

**TikTok sub-allocation (12,500 THB):**
- In-Feed Ads (awareness): 9,000 THB
- Spark Ads (boost organic posts): 3,500 THB

### 4. Content Calendar — 4-Week Structure

**Week 1: Launch / Awareness**
- Hero campaign video: "This is not makeup. This is your skin." (TikTok + IG Reels)
- Brand introduction post (IG Feed)
- "Why I stopped wearing heavy foundation" POV (TikTok)
- Paid: TikTok In-Feed Awareness Ads go live

**Week 2: Education / Proof**
- Foundation demo video on 3 different skin tones
- "Before/After — Same face, different confidence" content
- LGBTQ+ creator feature (partnership or UGC request)
- IG Stories: Shade finder quiz / poll
- Paid: Meta Traffic campaign starts (Shopee/Lazada links)

**Week 3: Social Proof / Community**
- Repost/react to first UGC and comments
- "Real people, real skin" compilation
- LGBTQ+ Pride angle content (authenticity, self-expression)
- TikTok Duet/Stitch campaign: "Show us your skin"
- Paid: Meta Retargeting begins (video viewers from Week 1–2)

**Week 4: Conversion Push**
- "Last chance this month" limited offer (if applicable)
- "Where to buy" explainer (Shopee, Lazada, Meta Shop links clearly shown)
- Testimonial roundup video
- IG Stories countdown + swipe-to-Shopee
- Paid: Increase Meta conversion budget; deploy Creative Reserve on best-performing content

### 5. Conversion Flow (No-Website Setup)

```
TikTok organic/paid → Profile bio link → Shopee/Lazada product page
TikTok Spark Ads   → Direct to Shopee/Lazada (link in ad)
IG Reels/Feed      → Profile bio link → Linktree (Shopee | Lazada | Meta Shop)
IG Stories         → Swipe-up → Shopee/Lazada
FB/IG Ads          → Meta Shop catalog OR Shopee/Lazada external link
Meta Shop          → Direct checkout (lowest friction)
```

Key decision: Use a **Linktree or equivalent** as the single link in bio on both TikTok and Instagram, directing to: (1) Meta Shop, (2) Shopee, (3) Lazada — with Shopee as the recommended default (highest Thai user base).

## KPIs (Testing Decisions)

A good KPI only measures outcomes the content is actually responsible for — not vanity metrics.

### Awareness (TikTok + IG Reels)
- Video views (target: 50,000+ total in Month 1)
- View-through rate > 50% on first 6 seconds
- Profile visits from content

### Engagement / Consideration (Meta + TikTok)
- Saves + Shares (stronger signal than Likes — indicates purchase intent)
- Comment sentiment (positive: "where to buy?" = conversion signal)
- Story poll participation rate

### Conversion (All Channels)
- Link clicks to Shopee/Lazada/Meta Shop
- Meta Shop add-to-cart and purchases
- Shopee/Lazada: track UTM parameters on all links to attribute sales

### Efficiency
- Cost per link click < 3–5 THB (Meta)
- Cost per 1,000 reach < 50 THB (TikTok awareness)
- ROAS target: ≥ 2x on Meta conversion campaigns by Week 3

## Out of Scope

- Website development or landing pages
- Influencer partnership fees (if talent is needed, content production budget must be reallocated)
- SEO or Google Ads
- Content for Facebook Groups or LINE OA
- Shopee/Lazada in-platform advertising (separate budget not included)
- Photography/video production beyond 8,000 THB production budget
- Month 2 retargeting or loyalty campaigns

---

## Further Notes

- All paid ads must comply with Meta's and TikTok's beauty/cosmetic advertising policies (no misleading before/after framing in paid units)
- LGBTQ+ audience targeting: use interest-based targeting (Pride, LGBTQ+ community, drag, makeup artists) — do not use demographic assumptions
- Shopee Thailand has strong beauty category momentum; prioritize Shopee over Lazada if only one link can be featured
- Creative reserve (2,000 THB) should be held until Week 2 data shows which organic content resonates — boost only proven winners
